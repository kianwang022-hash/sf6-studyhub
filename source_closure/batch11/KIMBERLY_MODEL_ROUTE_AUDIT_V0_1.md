Evidence:

- current Training Order starts with L Vagabond and Shadow Slide carry.
- R01/R02/R06/R14/R17/R20 repeatedly use Sprint -> Shadow Slide to move the opponent.
- Bomb appears only after corner and Sprint fundamentals in the published Training Order.

Judgment: **SUPPORTED**.

Evidence:

- Bomb section focuses combo / setplay / low / throw / safe jump / reversal-bait.
- Year 4 max stock increase to 3 expands advanced combo/setplay/lethal possibilities.

Judgment: **SUPPORTED**.

## Claim 3 — Kimberly differs from Rashid

Rashid:
movement/wind changes re-entry angles after stable Oki.

Kimberly:
movement converts ordinary contact into corner position; corner Bomb then amplifies that positional advantage.

Judgment: **SUPPORTED**.

- Shadow Slide simple dash Oki;

Judgment: **PASS**.
