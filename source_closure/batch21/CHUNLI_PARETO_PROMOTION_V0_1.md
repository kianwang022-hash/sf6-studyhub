# Chun-Li Pareto Promotion v0.1

Status: PHASE F PASS  
Goal: learner value, not route count.

## 1. S0 survivors

### Stable medium confirm
Promote:
- R01: 5MP > 2MP > M Spinning Bird Kick.
- O01/O02: +34 -> dash +15 -> 5MP / throw / shimmy.

Why:
- current training order begins here.
- this creates a complete ordinary round before stance complexity.
- 5MP Block +1 also teaches a real pressure owner.

### Stable low confirm
Promote:
- R02: 2LK > 2LP > L Spinning Bird Kick.

Why:
- minimum low confirm.
- establishes that SBK is a confirmed ender, not raw pressure.

### Anti-air
Promote:
- H Tensho Kicks as the first simple anti-air owner.
- teach grounded-vs-airborne Oki split only after the input is stable.

### Character-specific stance floor
Promote exactly one proof:
- R04: 2HP > Serenity Stream > Forward Strike > M Spinning Bird Kick.

Why:
- this is the smallest route that proves Chun-Li's unique “temporary stance as route compiler / charge bypass” identity.
- it does not force the learner to memorize a high/low/throw tree.
- it remains tied to a confirmed cancel entry.

### Stance exit
Expose:
- O21: Serenity Stream -> Up exit.

Why:
- current Year4 makes “do not overcommit” part of the minimum stance mental model.
