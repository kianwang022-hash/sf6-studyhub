## 6. Early evidence pattern

The current route/Oki material repeatedly shows two layers:

1. **ordinary shoto layer** — low/medium confirms, fireball, anti-air, H Shoryuken +30, strike/throw/shimmy;
2. **option-density layer** — charged fireball, Demon Raid, air fireball, Adamant Flame, teleport and high-reward setplay.

The 9000-health constraint makes bad option selection more expensive. This supports a provisional model where Akuma's identity is not 'always do more things', but 'use a larger option set without losing the stable shoto skeleton'.
