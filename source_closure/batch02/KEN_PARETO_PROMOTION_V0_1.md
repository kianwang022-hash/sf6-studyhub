# Ken Pareto Promotion v0.1

Status: **PHASE F CANDIDATE**

Goal: keep only routes with distinct decision value. Route length or damage alone is not a reason to expose a route.

## S0 minimum identity

Keep:

1. R02 — 2MP > 5LK > H Shoryuken
   - value: ordinary grounded hit -> stable KD +25 -> dash +6.
2. R04 — 2LK > 2LP > 5LK > H Shoryuken
   - value: low/light starter reuses the same stable next loop.
3. R01 — j.HP > 5MP > 5HP > Quick Dash Tatsu
   - value: first explicit Ken-specific choice: trade a heavier contact into screen carry rather than only damage.
4. P01 — +25 -> dash +6
   - value: first complete strike / throw / shimmy next-turn package.

Why this is S0: the learner can already play a whole round with shoto fundamentals, while one carry route prevents Ken from collapsing into a generic Ryu clone.

## S1 fundamentals

Promote:

- R08 shimmy PC -> +25 family.
- R09 blocked-reversal punish -> +25 family.
- R17 corner 2HP -> Jinrai HK -> L Shoryuken, conditioned corner.
- P09 corner forward throw +20 family, once corner control is stable.

Keep backend / Reference:

- alternative punish routes that end in the same +25 family without distinct execution/value.

## S2 structured match play

Promote:

- R05 2MK DRC easy carry default.
- R07 2MK DRC damage alternative.
- R11 corner Drive Reversal punish.
- R12 corner DI wall-splat stable route.
- R13 midscreen DI carry default.
- R15 back-to-corner DI position route.

Why: S2 adds Hit / Block / Whiff and screen-position decisions, so DRC and DI branches now have a real decision context.

## S3 mature choice

Promote selectively:

- R06 Year4 DRC carry optimization when extra execution buys a meaningful carry / SA state.
- R10 high-value PC carry.
- Quick Dash Forward Step Kick as a spacing tool, not a combo route.
- Jinrai branch family only after branch-by-branch practical value is explicit.
- H Dragonlash only as conditioned approach/pressure, never as universal plus-entry.

Core S3 decision:

same opening -> stable +6 loop / carry / corner +5 family / damage / stop.

## S4 high-value optimization

Promote after closure:

- one SA1 cashout family.
- one SA2 family only after R20 naming/input closure.
- 2-3 SA3 conversions chosen by real starter coverage.
- P07 / P08 only after exact Training Mode condition closure.

## Backend-only

- duplicate routes whose only difference is a small damage change.
- R16 approximate-damage version.
- R19/R20 until naming closure.
- narrow active-frame setups without exact condition truth.

## Pareto result

24 conversion candidates do **not** become 24 learner routes.
The learner surface should expose roughly 3-4 choices per high-frequency opportunity and often only 1-2.
