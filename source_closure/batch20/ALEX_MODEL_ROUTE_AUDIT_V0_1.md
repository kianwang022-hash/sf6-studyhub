# Alex Character Model ↔ Route Audit v0.1

Status: PHASE H PASS

## Claim 1 — ordinary strike/Oki must precede the full stance tree

Evidence:
- current light / 5MP routes are simple and reliable.
- H/OD Knee and forward throw produce concrete next-turn states.
- 4MK is currently +2 on block.
- Prowler entries are strongest when earned from M Flash Axe, 2HP, 2HK hit, charged buttons or knockdown/setplay.

Judgment: SUPPORTED.

Alex should not enter S0 as a full Prowler encyclopedia.

## Claim 2 — Alex is not merely another command-grab big body

Evidence:
- Year4 explicitly strengthened normal contact and 4MK plus pressure.
- 2HK received a direct Prowler transition.
- Prowler itself contains plus strike, overhead, low, throw and specialized defensive-read branches.
- current combo/Oki material repeatedly routes ordinary contact into stance/next-turn choice.

Judgment: SUPPORTED.

The distinct center is choice-denial layering after earned contact, not raw Power Bomb frequency.

## Claim 3 — Power Bomb becomes valuable after respect, not because +2 guarantees it

Evidence:
- 4MK is +2 on block.
- H Power Bomb is 5F, but opponent options such as jump/backwalk/reversal make the throw a read rather than a guaranteed continuation.
- current Oki material presents strike / throw / block / movement alternatives around these states.

Judgment: SUPPORTED.

Learner wording must explicitly preserve read/respect semantics.

## Claim 4 — Prowler must be taught as earned entry

Evidence:
- M Flash Axe hit +39 is a recurring stance gateway.
- 2HP and Year4 2HK can feed Prowler.
- charged buttons create additional stance paths.
- Prowler cannot be reduced to “sit in stance and wait”; its value comes from entering while the opponent must answer a branch.

Judgment: SUPPORTED.

## S0 identity gate

Accepted S0:

- 2LK > 2LP > L Flash Axe.
- 5MP > H Aerial Knee Smash.
- M Aerial Knee Smash anti-air.
- 4MK Block +2 -> 5LP first; Power Bomb shown only as respect/read.
- 2MP > M Flash Axe > Prowler -> Heavy Lariat as one earned stance branch.

Judgment: PASS.

This cannot be copied to Manon/Lily by renaming specials. The earned Prowler entry and +2 bridge are specifically Alex.

## Transfer check

Transfers in:
- big-body patience;
- respect -> command-grab feedback;
- strike/throw/anti-air/Oki.

New:
- earned-entry Prowler.
- stance high / low / throw / plus-strike branch tree.
- Year4 2HK -> Prowler and strengthened 4MK +2 pressure.
- route choice among damage / side switch / stance / Oki.

Unlearn:
- command grab first.
- plus means guaranteed throw.
- stance is a passive neutral mode.
- every knockdown means close repeat pressure.

## Audit outcome

Character Model v0 survives route/Oki evidence.

Freeze the learner center:

stable contact -> knockdown / +2 pressure -> strike earns respect -> Power Bomb or earned Prowler -> opponent escapes grab/stance -> strike/anti-air rises -> corner or reset.

Proceed to S0–S4, learner terminology and Role/Practical compilation.
