## 1. Current-version boundary

Alex released before the current Year 4 balance pass, so pre-August guides are not sufficient for numeric truth.

The 2026-08-03 adjustment materially changed the current decision model:

- 5MP hit advantage increased from +3 to +4.
- 2MK hit advantage increased from +4 to +5.
- 2HK can transition to Prowler on hit.
- 4MK / Oblique Stomp block advantage increased from +1 to +2.
- Prowler MP punish-counter Drive damage was increased.
- charged Prowler HP armor duration was extended.
- Dangerous Armbar gained projectile invulnerability during frames 3–22 and a larger throw hitbox.
- H Power Bomb gained a larger throw hitbox.
- Flying Cross Chop received new air-route utility.

These are not cosmetic buffs. They increase Alex's ability to create ordinary respect first and then force a wider strike / stance / command-grab decision.
