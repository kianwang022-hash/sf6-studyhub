# Alex Pareto Promotion v0.1

Status: PHASE F PASS  
Goal: learner value, not route count.

## 1. S0 survivors

### Light confirm
Promote:
- R02: 2LK > 2LP > L Flash Axe.

Why:
- low starter;
- stable;
- low cognitive cost;
- teaches ordinary contact before stance gambling.

Backend only at S0:
- extra light variants that solve the same problem.

### Medium confirm
Promote:
- R05: 5MP > H Aerial Knee Smash.

Why:
- 5MP is current Year4 core contact.
- Knee creates a clean Alex knockdown identity.
- route is short enough to stabilize immediately.

### Anti-air
Promote:
- M Aerial Knee Smash as the first dedicated anti-air answer.

Why:
- character-specific and memorable;
- does not require the learner to understand Prowler yet.

### Plus-pressure identity
Promote:
- 4MK Block +2 -> 5LP first.

Expose, but label as read:
- 4MK Block +2 -> H Power Bomb only after opponent respect.

Why:
- this is the smallest complete form of Alex's strike -> respect -> command-grab feedback.
- teaching the grab without the strike first would reproduce the generic big-body trap.

### Prowler identity floor
Promote one earned entry:
- 2MP > M Flash Axe > Prowler -> Heavy Lariat.

Why:
- proves Alex has a stance identity without dumping the full stance tree.
- teaches the central rule: earn Prowler from contact.

Defer:
- overhead / low / throw stance branches.
