## 3. Charge-system distinction from Guile / Dee Jay

The transferable chapter skill remains:

> charge is stored while doing other actions.

Honda uses it differently:

- charge keeps Headbutt / Sumo Smash available;
- but the current Game Plan is not organized around projectile cadence;
- normal contact often routes into Sumo Dash / Taiho Cannon Lift / Sumo Smash;
- once the opponent respects the strike/meaty layer, **Oicho Throw** becomes the high-value answer.

Honda therefore bridges charge discipline into the later “respect -> command-grab” curriculum.

## 7. Character-model hypothesis

Honda is provisionally:

> preserve charge while playing ordinary ground, use Sumo Dash / Headbutt / Smash to create large knockdown budgets, then turn the opponent's respect into a strike-vs-Oicho Throw loop.

Charge is the enabling discipline; **respect conversion** is the character-specific payoff.

## 8. Important anti-template notes

- 2MK is not a normal cancellable DRC low.
- Oicho is not a guaranteed post-knockdown command grab; it becomes strong after the strike/meaty layer creates respect.
- +42 safe jump is tied to Teppo/DI-specific states, not every Honda knockdown.
- Sumo Spirit is a later state layer that changes Hundred Hand Slap properties.
- current OD Teppo +3 is point-blank / setup-specific and should not replace basic Oicho learning.
