# Dhalsim Pareto Promotion v0.1

Status: PHASE F PASS  
Goal: teach range-rule reinterpretation and offense conversion without turning S0 into a Float/Teleport encyclopedia.

## 1. S0 survivors

### Ground geometry
Promote:
- Yoga Fire as the first projectile lane.
- 5MP as a long mid-range probe.
- 2MK as a low/slide geometry tool with explicit late-contact caveat.

Why:
- the learner must immediately understand that Dhalsim plays space differently from ordinary footsies.
- frame advantage is not taught as one fixed number at every range.

### Anti-air lanes
Promote:
- 4MP Yoga Uppercut for close/steep jump.
- 5HK for farther jump lane.

Why:
- “one anti-air button” would misrepresent his geometry.
- only two lanes are needed in S0; advanced air conversions are deferred.

### Stable close confirm
Promote:
- R04: 4MK > M Yoga Flame (+33).

Why:
- proves Dhalsim has a simple close-range turn and does not need Float/Teleport to function.

### Character-specific transition floor
Promote exactly one Drive route:
- R03: 2LP > CDR > 5LP > 4MP > M Yoga Blast (+42) > immediate forward P Yoga Teleport -> point-blank +3.

Why:
- this is the minimum complete expression of Dhalsim identity: remote/control result becomes close offense.
- raw Teleport remains excluded.

## 2. S1 survivors

Promote:
- Yoga Arch delayed geometry.
- forward throw +31 -> DR 2MK pressure.
- R06: Aerial Yoga Float > j.HP > 1HP > M Yoga Flame.
- R05: 2MP CDR -> 4MK -> H Yoga Flame +45.
- M/H/L Blast role distinctions.

Why:
- S1 adds a second spatial dimension (delayed arc / Float) only after the learner can already control ground and cash one knockdown into offense.

## 3. S2 survivors

Promote:
- Year4 OD Fire -> Arch -> Comet.
- Year4 OD Arch -> Fire -> Comet.
- corner 4MK > H Blast > L Blast +37.
- Teleport/Float branch choices after earned knockdown.
- anti-air conversion routes.
- one OD Flame route.

Why:
- by S2 the learner can combine geometry with position and resources.

## 4. S3 survivors

Promote:
- corner +37 point-blank / shimmy / L Fire setplay package.
- corner forward throw +31 meaty package.
- active H Yoga Flame plus setup.
- OD Yoga Flame +51.
- spacing/late-contact optimization for 2MK / 2HK.
- exact projectile coverage into Teleport/Float.

Why:
- these require precise active timing / spacing ownership and should not define the first playable round.

## 5. S4 survivors

Promote only when useful:
- SA cashout.
- matchup-specific projectile geometry.
- advanced Float / Drill / Comet heights.
- Teleport cross-up / side-switch lab.
- Burnout corner sequences.
- character-specific limb hurtbox / DI counterplay.
