## 3. Bayani truth

Bayani is the character's defining state cycle.

Current evidence supports:

- normal Alon hit can grant Bayani;
- Talim ng Hangin hit and SA1 are also acquisition routes in current guide notes;
- while Bayani is active, Alon upgrades to Boosted Alon;
- normal Alon is highly punishable on block (around -12);
- Boosted Alon variants become much safer on block:
  - L around -1;
  - M around -2;
  - H around -3;
  - OD around -1;
- Boosted Alon normally consumes Bayani on hit/block;
- OD Boosted Alon can re-acquire Bayani on hit in the current system;
- during SA2 Nakatagong Lakas, Yasmine has persistent Bayani for about 25 seconds.

This is a **state cycle**, not a Juri-style multi-stock inventory.

## 5. Current Game Plan evidence

Repeated current structure:

ground contact -> normal Alon -> gain Bayani -> next Boosted Alon -> stronger/safe state -> +42 safe jump / high-damage conversion -> reacquire Bayani -> corner pressure -> SA2 persistent Bayani when resources justify it

This supports a cyclical state model rather than a one-time install.

## 6. Important distinction from Juri

Juri:
- builds discrete stock;
- chooses when to spend stock on several Boosted specials.

Yasmine:
- enters a binary/temporary Bayani state;
- Bayani specifically changes Alon into Boosted Alon;
- Boosted Alon typically consumes the state;
- the loop is about reacquiring and reusing Bayani;
- SA2 temporarily suspends the normal acquire/consume rhythm by providing persistent Bayani.
