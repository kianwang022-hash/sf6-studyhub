## 3. Psycho Mine lifecycle truth

Current structured evidence supports:

- normal Backfist Combo hit plants Psycho Mine;
- Mine remains on the opponent for a limited time;
- Mine can self-detonate after its timer;
- another Backfist Combo, Psycho Crusher, or Devil Reverse can detonate it on hit/block;
- Mine-enhanced versions have different damage / block advantage / knockdown properties.

This is a stateful opponent-mark system, not a stackable inventory.

## 6. Current Game Plan evidence

The maintained guide repeatedly shows:

ground contact -> Backfist Combo -> Psycho Mine planted -> H Backfist +31 / DR Oki -> overhead / low / throw / shimmy -> Mine-enhanced Backfist / Crusher -> plus-frame or +42+ state -> bigger combo/corner pressure

This supports a state-cycle model:

1. stable normal-state route plants Mine;
2. Mine presence changes the opponent's risk when blocking key specials;
3. Bison chooses when/how to detonate the Mine;
4. after detonation, return to a normal-state route and replant later.

## 7. Charge-system distinction

Bison inherits charge storage for Psycho Crusher / Shadow Rise, but charge is not the whole identity.

- Backfist Combo and Double Knee Press do not require the same charge state.
- Mine lets Bison transform otherwise unsafe Psycho Crusher into plus pressure.
- therefore charge availability and Mine state interact: the learner must know both **can I use Crusher now?** and **what will Crusher mean in the current Mine state?**

## 8. Important anti-template notes

- Mine-enhanced values must never appear without Mine state.
- normal Psycho Crusher is highly unsafe on block.
- normal Backfist is unsafe on block even though it plants Mine on hit.
- +42 safe jump belongs to Mine-enhanced Backfist/Crusher states, not generic Bison knockdown.
- Shadow Rise / Devil Reverse height-dependent frame data remains conditioned.
- SA2 / high-resource Mine routes are late.
