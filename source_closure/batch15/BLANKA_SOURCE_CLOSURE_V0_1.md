## 3. Charge-system distinction

Blanka inherits the chapter skill:

> charge is stored while doing other actions.

But charge is only one axis of his kit:

- [4]6P Rolling Attack gives travel / route / spacing options;
- [2]8K Vertical Rolling Attack gives anti-air / route Oki;
- Electric Thunder is a non-charge special and is the most common early Oki ender;
- Coward Crouch / Hop / Backstep / Bomb create later setup layers.

Therefore the learner should not reduce Blanka to “hold charge and ball.”
