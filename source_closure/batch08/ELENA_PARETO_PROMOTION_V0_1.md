## S0 minimum playable

Keep:

1. R01 — light -> L Spinning Scythe
   - value: simple confirm / +34.
2. R04 — 2MK > 2LP > L Spinning Scythe
   - value: low confirm / same +34 loop.
3. R05 — 5MP target -> M Rhino Horn
   - value: medium contact / +39.
4. 5MK block +1
   - value: first ordinary strike/throw respect point.
5. anti-air 2HP / Scratch Wheel role.

Why:
Elena must first have a stable grounded contact -> knockdown -> simple next turn. Her defining mix hub can enter after the learner has an actual setup that earns it.
