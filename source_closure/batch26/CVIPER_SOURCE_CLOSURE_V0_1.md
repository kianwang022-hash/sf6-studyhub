## 1. Current-version boundary

Current Year4 sources establish several decision-changing facts:

- standing MK is currently Hit +5, making meterless link routes and HJC conversion easier.
- crouching MK is currently Hit +5 / Block -1 and is High Jump Cancelable.
- Light Thunder Dash currently reaches Hit +2..+4 and can Punish Counter into follow-up normals at spacing.
- OD Thunder Dash is -2 on block and gives a +42 knockdown.
- OD Seismic Hammer is currently -8 on block and supports current route families such as OD Seismic Hammer -> 2HK and delayed HJC conversions.
- almost every standard normal except 2HK can High Jump Cancel; HJC can itself special-cancel during pre-jump, effectively turning normals that are not ordinary-special-cancelable into route starters.

The teaching consequence is important: C. Viper is not merely “an execution-heavy character”. Her unique grammar is that normal contact can be re-routed through High Jump Cancel into specials, movement and setplay.
