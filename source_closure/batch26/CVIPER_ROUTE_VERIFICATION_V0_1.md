# C. Viper Route Verification v0.1

Status: PHASE E PASS  
Sources: SRC03 / SRC05 / SRC06

## Verified ordinary truths

- 5MP is Block +1.
- R01: light -> M Thunder Dash is a simple meterless confirm.
- R05: Year4 5MK +5 links to 5LK -> M Thunder Dash.
- forward throw is +28.
- current OD Seismic Hammer Block is -8.

## HJC-conditioned routes

R06/R07/R12/R15/R18/R19/R21/R25 require:
- declared HJC-capable source move;
- 1 Drive ownership outside SA1 install;
- valid pre-jump special cancel;
- the declared hit/block state.

2HK and unique attacks are not covered by the standard-normal HJC rule.

## Thunder Dash / Tracer conditions

- H Thunder Dash is -24 on block: all learner H Dash routes remain confirmed.
- Tracer Combination consumes Drive and is unavailable after OD Thunder Dash.
- L/M/H Thunder Dash -> Tracer create different knockdown budgets; never merge them.
- R02/O01 use the H Dash -> Tracer +25..26 owner only.

## Burning Kick branch audit

- Burning Kick hit -> Knuckled Pursuit is the hit-conversion branch.
- Burning Kick block -> Double Burn is the pressure branch; Double Burn Block +2.
- Double Burn is not a hit follow-up default.
- aerial Burning Kick values remain height / spacing conditioned.

## Seismic / exact-state audit

- Seismic Hammer is a low projectile-like special with +53 limited-juggle state.
- R23 +45 and R24 +42 are exact Seismic-air routes.
- O12 +44 is an exact H Burning Kick height/route family.
- R11 / O20 +42 values retain their OD / route owner.
- active-frame 5MP meaties require the declared whiff/timing package.

## SA1

SA1 install may remove Drive cost from High Jump / special follow-ups during install. This does not retroactively make normal HJC free outside install.

## Conflicts / provisional

Burning Kick height, character-specific safe-jump hurtboxes and advanced feint timings remain Training Mode / S4 material.
