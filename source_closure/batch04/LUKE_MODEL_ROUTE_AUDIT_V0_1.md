# Luke Character Model ↔ Route Audit v0.1

Status: **PHASE H PRE-AUDIT PASS**

## Claim 1 — Luke is playable before Perfect Flash Knuckle

Evidence:

- R02/R03 both reach +36 via ordinary L Flash Knuckle.
- P01 creates a complete strike / throw / shimmy loop from +36.
- R12 gives a simple meter route into DDT +12.
- Current training order explicitly places stable routes before Perfect timing.

Judgment: **SUPPORTED**.

## Claim 2 — Luke's unique delta is execution-investment choice

Evidence:

- stable L Flash +36 is already strong and low-load.
- DDT +12 gives a different easy hard-KD result.
- point-blank +7 No Chaser and +64 H Flash packages offer stronger setplay at higher execution / condition cost.
- Perfect Flash Knuckle variants appear after the stable route layer and can improve route quality.

Judgment: **SUPPORTED**.

## Claim 3 — route quality should be compared by end state, not difficulty

Evidence:

- +36, +12, +7, +28…31, +64 all create materially different next turns.
- the hardest route is not always the best practical answer.
- current guide's own training order prioritizes stability before Perfect timing.

Judgment: **SUPPORTED**.

## S0 identity gate

Proposed S0:

- normal midrange / Sand Blast / anti-air;
- 2MP and low/light -> L Flash +36;
- immediate PDR -> one fixed strike;
- one stable jump-in route;
- explicit defer of Perfect timing.

Question: does Luke still feel like Luke without Perfect timing in S0?

Judgment: **YES**. The stable Flash Knuckle -> Oki rhythm is already character-specific. Perfect timing deepens route quality later.
