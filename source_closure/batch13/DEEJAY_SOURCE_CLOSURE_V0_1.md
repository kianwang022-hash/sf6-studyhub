## 3. Charge-system distinction from Guile

The transferable charge skill remains:

> charge is stored while doing other actions.

But Dee Jay uses that skill differently.

Guile:
- charge availability directly owns the Boom / Somersault neutral shell.

Dee Jay:
- charge keeps Air Slasher / Jackknife available;
- **L Air Slasher itself is a fake projectile**, so charge can create timing ambiguity;
- non-charge Sobat / Machine Gun Uppercut / Jus Cool routes mean he can intentionally break the opponent's expectation that “charge character = charge move next”.

Therefore the new emphasis is not merely charge preservation, but **rhythm deception around charge availability**.

Dee Jay is provisionally:

> a charge character who uses charge availability to make timing harder to read, then converts one uncertain defensive beat into very high reward. His neutral shell is not “Guile but faster”; he alternates real/fake projectile timing, positive st.MP contact, non-charge Sobat routes and Jus Cool branches.
