# A.K.I. Pareto Promotion v0.1

Status: PHASE F PASS  
Goal: teach state conversion without making poison bookkeeping the price of entry.

## 1. S0 survivors

### Stable confirm / poison application
Promote:
- R02: 2LK > 2LP > 2LP > H Serpent Lash.
- R03: 5MK > 5MP > H Serpent Lash.

Why:
- both end in the same ordinary +44 state.
- they teach that the first job is simply: get a normal opening, apply poison / establish the state, and take Oki.

### Core +44 Oki
Promote:
- O01: +44 -> two dashes -> about +6.
- low / 5HK / throw as a tiny first-choice set.
- shimmy only as the opponent begins throw-teching.

Why:
- current game plan explicitly makes H Lash +44 the bridge from poison application into the real offense loop.

### Anti-air
Promote:
- H Serpent Lash as the first anti-air owner, without poison-burst extension in S0.

### Poison identity floor
Expose one sentence:
- "If the opponent is already poisoned, the next Lash may burst and change the route. S0 does not optimize it yet."

Why:
- the learner must understand why the poison icon matters, but does not need two route books on day one.

## 2. S1 survivors

Promote:
- R13: poisoned M Serpent Lash -> +69 burst route.
- R12: poisoned H Lash burst -> return to +44.
- O09: Toxic Wreath +40 pressure.
- DR low / overhead / 2MK entries.
- 6HK Block +2 as simple ordinary pressure.

Why:
- S1 introduces the first explicit state-dependent choice: apply poison vs cash poisoned state into a burst conversion.

## 3. S2 survivors

Promote:
- R14/R23: poisoned M Lash -> corner Orchid Spring puddle route.
- O10/O11: +38 puddle pressure and opponent escape behavior.
- R22/O22: current Year4 OD Chaser block-poison with Drive Parry exception.
- Qiong Qi unpoisoned +34 vs poisoned +54.
- one Sinister Slide / Heel Strike route only when it owns a clear position/carry role.

Why:
- by S2 the learner can reason about "state + position", not merely poisoned/not-poisoned.

## 4. S3 survivors

Promote:
- O03-O06: +18 -> 6HP active-meaty family with predecessor-state ownership.
- O07/O08: H Lash safe-jump -> shimmy family, spacing conditioned.
- OD Cruel Fate hit +42 vs block +2.
- OD Serpent Lash poisoned +71 as a value-comparison branch.
- poison-puddle projectile meaty +12 family.

Why:
- these are exact-state setplay and resource choices, not beginner identity.
