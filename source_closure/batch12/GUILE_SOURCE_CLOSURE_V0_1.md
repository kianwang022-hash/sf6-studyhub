## 3. Charge-system curriculum authority

This chapter introduces a new transferable skill:

> Charge is stored while doing other actions; it is not a separate waiting phase.

For Guile this means the learner must deliberately preserve back/down-back charge while:

- crouch-blocking;
- using compatible crouching normals;
- using charge-preserving back-direction normals;
- waiting for a jump;
- deciding between Sonic Boom and Somersault Kick.

The learner should not be taught “crouch and wait until charge is ready.”

## 7. Character-model hypothesis

Guile is provisionally:

> a charge character whose strength comes from keeping Sonic Boom / Somersault availability alive while still moving through normal SF6 decisions. The new skill is charge preservation inside ordinary neutral and pressure, not passive waiting.

Sonic Boom shapes horizontal movement; Somersault makes jumping risky; grounded normals and charge-preserving routes cash out the hesitation.

## 8. Important anti-template notes

- Guile's 2MK is **non-cancelable**; do not inherit Ryu/Ken/Luke 2MK DRC semantics.

- +42 safe jump is not a universal H Somersault result; hit height matters.
- OD Sonic Blade +1 is a Year 4 pressure upgrade but belongs after basic charge discipline.
- SA2 Solid Puncher is a late system layer, not S0 identity.
