# Ed Pareto Promotion v0.1

Status: PHASE F PASS  
Goal: preserve Ed's midrange identity without turning S0 into a Flicker/charge encyclopedia.

## 1. S0 survivors

### Stable close confirm
Promote:
- R01: 2LP > 5LP > M Psycho Blitz.
- R02: 5MP > 2LK > M Psycho Blitz.

Why:
- both end in M Blitz +39.
- they build one stable knockdown owner.

### Midrange identity
Promote:
- R04: confirmed 5MK > L Psycho Flicker.

Why:
- 5MK is one of Ed's defining long midrange contacts.
- the row explicitly teaches that L Flicker is only submitted on hit / whiff punish because Block -6.
- this proves Ed is not just a close-range boxer.

### Re-entry
Promote:
- O01: M Blitz +39 -> Drive Rush +8 -> 5HP / 2MK / throw / shimmy.

Why:
- this is the smallest complete “range touch -> knockdown -> re-entry” loop.

### Anti-air
Promote:
- L Psycho Uppercut as the first simple anti-air owner; deeper air routes later.

Defer:
- charged Flicker.
- Psycho Knuckle.
- Kill Switch carry.
- Spark/Shoot layering.
- SA2.
